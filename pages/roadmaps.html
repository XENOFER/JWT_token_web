Learning roadmaps

Same destination — confident, secure use of JSON Web Tokens — but the route depends on why you're here. Pick the track that matches your goal and follow it step by step. Every step links straight to the chapter or resource you need next.

Track 01

Beginner

Never used JWTs. Goal: read and reason about any token.

 Track 02

API Security

Building auth. Goal: ship a correct, hardened implementation.

 Track 03

Advanced / Offensive

Testing & defending. Goal: assess real systems safely.

For: developers new to tokens. Outcome: you can open any JWT, explain its three parts, and describe how a login uses it.

Auth Flow

Track 03 — Advanced / Offensive

For: security engineers and pentesters. Outcome: you can find and demonstrate JWT flaws — and explain the fixes — on systems you're authorized to test.

Step 1 · Map the attacks

Vulnerabilities

The full catalogue: algorithm, header-injection, claim, and storage flaws.

Step 2 · Practise safely

Attack Demos

Run none, weak-secret, tampering and expiry against fabricated tokens.

Step 3 · Go deeper

Algorithm confusion

Understand RS→HS confusion and header-key abuse at the mechanism level.

Step 4 · Lab it

Work through realistic, deliberately vulnerable targets in a legal sandbox.

Step 5 · Tooling

Add a dedicated toolkit for tampering and exploitation during authorized assessments.

Step 6 · Authoritative guidance

RFC 8725 & OWASP

Ground your findings and recommendations in the Best Current Practices and OWASP guidance.
